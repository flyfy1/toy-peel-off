📱 Peel Off: Peel the film off a brand-new phone. Then try to put the film down.

A new phone in its box, factory film still on. Take the corner tab and pull: the film curls away with a fine crackle and the screen comes out clean. Then a camera, a watch, a car screen, a microwave, the TV remote your mum said to leave alone, and more. A tiny satisfying toy, no sign-up.

How to play

  1. Take the yellow tab at the corner of the film with a finger or the mouse and drag it across the screen. The film curls back over itself with a fine crackle of tiny ticks; the direction you pull steers the fold.
  2. Pull most of the way and it zips off the rest by itself. Let go too early and it falls back down, trapping a few new air bubbles; take the tab and pull again.
  3. The film comes off as a crumpled wad that clings to your finger with static. Shake it off with a quick back-and-forth, or tap the tray beside the object.
  4. Then the next thing arrives. There are ten in all: a phone, a steel panel, a TV remote in its plastic (Mum has opinions), a camera, a new watch, a car screen, a microwave, a fridge door, a laptop and a kitchen cabinet. Tap the counter at the top to pick one.

FAQ

Can I peel protective film online?

Yes. Peel Off at peel.integ.life is a free browser toy. Take the tab at the corner of the film with your finger or mouse and drag it across. The film curls away with sound and, on Android, vibration. Nothing to install and no account.

What can I peel?

Ten things: a new phone, the blue film on a stainless-steel panel, a TV remote kept in its plastic, the camera on the back of a phone, a new watch, a car's centre screen, a microwave panel, a fridge door, a laptop lid and a kitchen cabinet door. They come round in turn, and you can pick one by tapping the counter. Each film behaves a little differently.

Why did the film on the microwave leave a strip behind?

Because that edge is tucked under the trim, as on a real one. It stretches, tears, and the strip under the trim stays. That is the joke.

Why does the film go back down when I let go?

Because that is what a half-peeled film does. If you let go before about two thirds of it is off, it falls back and traps a few air bubbles. Take the tab and pull again; nothing is lost.

The film is stuck to my finger. How do I get rid of it?

That is static. Shake it off with a quick back-and-forth, or tap the tray beside the object. Sometimes it takes two tries.

Is Peel Off free?

Yes. It is free, with no sign-up, no download and no ads. It runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.

Does it have sound and vibration?

Yes. The crackle is synthesised live in the browser with the Web Audio API, one tiny tick each time the adhesive lets go, rising in pitch as the film comes off; there are no audio files to load. On Android phones the pull is also a fine buzz of vibration; iPhone browsers do not support web vibration.

Which languages does it support?

English, 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese), Español, Français and 日本語 (Japanese). The language is picked automatically from your browser settings; any other language falls back to English.
